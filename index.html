Biography

About

Wayne Martin Mellinger, Ph.D. is an independent sociologist, street outreach worker, and public writer based in Santa Barbara, California. His work centers on homelessness, social abandonment, and the structural forces that determine who lives and who dies in American cities.

Drawing on nearly two decades of immersion in Santa Barbara's homelessness services system, Mellinger brings together ethnomethodology, critical social theory, and street-level observation to develop what he calls Critical Interactionism — a framework that tracks how ordinary institutional encounters accumulate into life-and-death outcomes for the most vulnerable.

He holds a Ph.D. in Sociology from UC Santa Barbara (1990), where he trained under Don H. Zimmerman, Thomas P. Wilson, and Harvey Molotch in the Santa Barbara School of ethnomethodology and conversation analysis. His foundational empirical work includes peer-reviewed studies on paramedic dispatch calls, paramedic–nurse directive negotiation, and psychiatric interview repair sequences.

Mellinger maintains two research manuscript platforms: Doing Modernity: Studies in Critical Interactionism and The Dionysian Naturalist: Studies in Religious Naturalism and Other Emerging Nature Religions. He writes public-facing commentary for Noozhawk and organizes the annual Longest Night Homeless Memorial Vigil at the Santa Barbara County Courthouse.

Scholarship

Books & Manuscripts

Under Contract · Routledge

Abandonment in Real Time

Recognition, Rupture, and the Institutional Production of Homeless Death

Routledge Interactionist Currents

A thirteen-chapter monograph tracing how ordinary bureaucratic encounters — documentation requirements, eligibility screenings, service referrals — accumulate into what Mellinger calls "administrative death." The book develops a six-operation transformation chain from Conditional Recognition through Documentary Sorting, Temporal Mismatch, and beyond, using composite figures drawn from years of street outreach in Santa Barbara.

Manuscript in Progress

I Saw the Best Souls of My City

A Poetry Collection of Witness

Poetry · Five-Part Manuscript

A five-part collection moving from Witness through Lament, Remembrance, Awe, and Consecration. The poems bear witness to the unhoused people Mellinger has known through two decades of street outreach — their dignity, their deaths, and the indifferent systems that failed them. Formatted as a 6×9 trade paperback.

Research Manuscript Platform

Doing Modernity

Studies in Critical Interactionism

Independent Scholarly Platform

An ongoing platform developing Critical Interactionism as a theoretical framework — integrating ethnomethodology, conversation analysis, institutional ethnography (Dorothy Smith), and critical social theory (Wacquant, Mbembe, Agamben) into a sustained account of how interaction orders reproduce structural inequality.

Independent Scholarly Platform

A constructive theological project developing Dionysian Religious Naturalism (DRN) — a framework for sacred naturalism, embodied spirituality, and post-theistic religious life. Community Work

Advocacy & Street Outreach

For nearly two decades, Mellinger has worked not only as a scholar of homelessness but as a practitioner within Santa Barbara's service system — doing street outreach, serving on county commissions, and organizing community remembrance.

Street Outreach

Nearly twenty years of multi-role immersion in Santa Barbara's homelessness services system, working directly with unhoused individuals and observing the institutions that serve — and fail — them.

Behavioral Wellness Commission

Appointed member of the Santa Barbara County Behavioral Wellness Commission, providing citizen oversight and policy input on mental health and substance use services.

Continuum of Care

Participant in the Santa Maria/Santa Barbara County Continuum of Care — the regional planning body that coordinates federal homeless assistance funding and strategy.

Longest Night Vigil

Organizer of the annual Longest Night Homeless Memorial Vigil at the Santa Barbara County Courthouse, honoring community members who died while unhoused.

Santa Barbara Home Fund

Developer of a philanthropic proposal to establish the Santa Barbara Home Fund, presented to the Santa Barbara Foundation for institutional umbrella support.

Policy Advocacy

Author of formal policy memoranda on homeless mortality submitted to the Santa Barbara County Board of Supervisors and County CEO.
