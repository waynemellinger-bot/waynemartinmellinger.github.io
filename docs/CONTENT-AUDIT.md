## Site-level content questions

*October 8: items 1–3 below were corrected conservatively per the content review (date-neutral
Noozhawk wording; "helped establish"; "14th year" removed). Item 4 is now handled by labelling the
poems a selection. The seven date conflicts are shown publicly on their records. A new open item:
the Behavioral Wellness Commission (see DECISIONS-FOR-WAYNE.md, A0).*

1. **Noozhawk start date.** The biography says "Since 2012, he has written more than a
   hundred columns"; the CV says "Columnist — Noozhawk, 2012–present". Noozhawk's own
   archive has 133 columns, the first on June 8, 2011. The biography and CV are shown
   unchanged, because they are Wayne's text; Wayne should decide whether to correct them.
2. **Freedom Warming Centers.** The biography says Wayne "helped establish" them; the
   community list says "Co-founder". Both are shown as they were.
3. **Longest Night.** The CV says "Founder, Longest Night Memorial (14th year)". This will
   go out of date each December; consider "Founder, Longest Night Memorial (since 2013)"
   or similar, if that year is right.
4. **"Eighteen poems."** The old page introduced "Eighteen poems selected from *Moments of
   Awe*" but contained seventeen. The new page says "a working selection" without a number.
   Is a poem missing?
5. **"I Saw the Best Souls of My City"** is dated December 21, 2025 ("Final version, 13th
   Longest Night"). Is it in *Moments of Awe* (2025), as the poetry page implies, or later?
6. **Book titles.** *Abandonment in Real Time* has two subtitles (old site: "Knowledge,
   Care, and the Moral Limits of Recognition"; Wayne's notes: "Institutional Recognition and
   Premature Death Among the Homeless"). The old site lists a book manuscript "From Minds to
   Moments"; Wayne's notes describe *The Sociology of Interaction*. Same project or two?
7. **Portrait credits.** Five of the seven portraits are paintings or illustrations with no
   artist recorded. If any were made with an image generator, say so in the caption; if by an
   artist, credit them (`credit:` in `images.yml`).
8. **Prayers.** Three prayer-poems from *Moments of Awe* are named but not on the site
   ("A Prayer for Those Who Serve", "Prayer for Longest Night", "A Prayer for an Entheogenic
   Sacrament"). Add their texts, or remove the mention.
9. **Our Neighbors on the Streets** (Wayne's fourth platform) is mentioned but not linked or
   catalogued; its address could not be checked from here.
10. **Publicly visible personal history.** The biography (Wayne's own text, carried over
    unchanged) discusses incarceration, bipolar disorder and addiction; so do several poems.
    That was already public on the old site. Nothing new was added.
