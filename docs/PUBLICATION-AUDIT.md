# Publication-link audit

October 14, 2026. Covers every record in `src/_data/works/scholarship.yml` (the scholarly
bibliography). Noozhawk columns and platform essays already link to their originals and are
not repeated here.

**Method.** Each title was searched for a DOI, the publisher's article page, a stable repository
copy, or an authoritative bibliographic record, in that order of preference. A link was added
only where the address itself was found and its details matched the record. No DOI was built
from a pattern, and no citation detail was changed on the strength of a single unofficial
listing.

**Limits of this check.** Publisher pages (Wiley, Taylor & Francis, ScienceDirect) block
automated reading, so for those the confirmation is the publisher's own listing as indexed by
search (title, journal, volume and DOI shown together), plus, for *Negotiated Orders*, the EMCA
Wiki bibliography. Each linked address should get one click-through check by a person before
merging; that takes about two minutes.

**No master bibliography was found.** Searching Wayne's Google Drive found no document titled or
functioning as a master bibliography. The sources compared were: the previous site's
"Peer-Reviewed Publications" list, the EverybodyWiki biography's publication list, Wayne's
ResearchGate profile, PhilArchive, and the EMCA Wiki. If a master bibliography exists, the
unresolved items below should be reconciled against it.

## The three items flagged in the assignment

**John Henry Adams.** Missing from the previous site and from the October rebuild. The one
listing found gives "John Henry Adams and the Image of the New Negro", *The International
Review of African American Art*, 1997. That magazine is not indexed online for 1997, so the
details could not be checked. The record is now on the Publications page, honestly labelled.

**The two Los Angeles Times gang-murder analyses.** Both were already listed and are correctly
two separate works, not a duplicate: one on gang-murder coverage generally (*Cultural Studies:
A Research Annual*, 1997) and one on the Stephanie Kuhen case (*Human Systems*, 1997). Neither has
an online record with volume and pages. ResearchGate lists both, with "Newz" entered twice.
Wayne's 2012 Doing Modernity post on "Newz from the 'Hood" may give the citation; it could not be
opened from here.

## Publication types

The previous site put everything under "Peer-Reviewed Publications". The Publications page now
separates **Journal articles**, **Chapters in edited volumes and research annuals**, **Other
articles**, **Books** and **Forthcoming**. Newspaper commentary (Noozhawk) stays under Public
Scholarship & Media. Whether each venue was refereed was not checked; research annuals often
were not peer-reviewed in the journal sense. Restoring a "peer-reviewed" label to specific items
is Wayne's call.
